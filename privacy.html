Privacy Policy

What EEE Records keeps about you, who can see it, and what is sent outside this site. Last updated 23 November 2026.

EEE Records is a CSE 518 course project at the University of Chittagong, not an official university website. The marks, attendance and achievements shown are sample data, not real records.

What we keep

  • Your account: your university email, your role (student, teacher, office or examiner) and your password. The password is never stored as you typed it, only as a salted hash that can't be turned back into the password.
  • Your academic record: courses, class-test marks, attendance and results, as entered by your teachers and the academic office.
  • A history of every change: each mark or record change is written to a tamper-evident log with who made it and when, so changes can be checked later. Objections you raise stay in a mark's history, even after they are answered.
  • Things you send us: complaints, profile changes you ask for, and files you upload to your locker.
  • Sign-in safety: wrong-password attempts are counted per device so a device can be locked for a short time after too many tries.

Checking it's really you

When a student registers, we ask for their parents' names and compare them with the office's own record, in English or Bangla. They are used only for that check and never shown to anyone else.

Who can see your marks

You, the teacher of that course, and the academic office. Other students can't, and teachers can't see courses they don't teach.

Staying signed in

When you sign in, your browser keeps a sign-in ticket: for 30 days if you tick "Remember me", otherwise for 12 hours. Signing out removes it. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

What is sent outside this site

  • Email: verification codes, password-reset codes and notices about your record are sent to your email address.
  • The AI assistant (student page): when you ask it something, your question goes to an outside AI service (Groq, or Google Gemini as a backup) together with your first name, semester, lab group, this semester's courses and teachers, your weekly routine and upcoming class tests. It is not sent your marks, GPA, attendance, parents' names or anyone else's data. These services' free plans may use what they are sent to improve their products, which is why nothing more personal is sent. The assistant can only answer; it can't change any record.
